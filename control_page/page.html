    Predicted task

    Stream: frames sent per second vs the video's rate; upscaling time per frame; frame budget = share of each second spent working rather than waiting for the next frame slot (100% means the stream cannot keep up); late frames = sends that missed their slot. Gaze model: predictions per second (input is 30 Hz); model time in its process; round trip including the pipe; lag = how far the shown prediction is behind the newest frame; queue and drops if it falls behind. Threads & compute: CPU as a share of one core (the stream process includes all its threads; the model runs in its own process). MPS memory is GPU memory per process; GPU utilisation is not available on macOS without root. Updated twice a second.

    Head (live)

    az 0° · el 0°

    Where the viewer's head points; the gaze model relies mostly on its motion. Live (default): drag the pad (azimuth ±90°, elevation ±45°) or use ←→↑↓ (Shift for bigger steps); either switches the head to Live, and a head nobody moves stays at the centre. Trace: replays a head path from a CSV in Videos/. Changes apply on the next frame.

    Pipelines

    Video Super Resolution

    Gaze-Contingent Video Super Resolution

    How a frame moves through the stream, with live values. Every NDI source comes from the same decoded frames: Source sends them as they are; Bicubic upscales 4×; Foveated runs the SR model around the predicted gaze (the gaze point arrow, at the step where the gaze will be after k frames) on top of bicubic, or with SR off sends bicubic as is, and can draw the full horizon gaze prediction and the fovea outline; VSR runs the SR model on the whole frame of every k-th frame (keyframes), and RIFE makes the frames in between from the two upscaled keyframes around them (not used at k = 1). The gaze model runs in its own process on 30 Hz samples of the decoded frames and the head pose, in two parts: UniSal computes each sample's saliency map, and the LSTM predicts the next 20 gaze steps from the last 15 maps and head poses. The row under each box shows where that step runs (CPU, GPU, or NPU: the Apple Neural Engine) and in which thread or process. Press ▶ on an NDI box to stream that source alone (■ there stops it); while it streams, the parts it does not use are greyed out. Each area's options are under its diagram. Models stay loaded between runs.
