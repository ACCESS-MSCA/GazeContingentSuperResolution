Pick the NDI sources to send: all come from the same decoded frames and are sent in lockstep, frame for frame, so a run goes at the pace of its slowest source (bicubic and gaze are real time; foveated and VSR play in slow motion).     Predicted task

    Stream: frames sent per second vs the video's rate; upscaling time per frame; frame budget = share of each second spent working rather than waiting for the next frame slot (100% means the stream cannot keep up); late frames = sends that missed their slot. Gaze model: predictions per second (input is 30 Hz); model time in its process; round trip including the pipe; lag = how far the shown prediction is behind the newest frame; queue and drops if it falls behind. Threads & compute: CPU as a share of one core (the stream process includes all its threads; the model runs in its own process). MPS memory is GPU memory per process; GPU utilisation is not available on macOS without root. Updated twice a second.

    Pipeline

    How a frame moves through the stream, with live values. Every NDI source comes from the same decoded frames: Source sends them as they are; Bicubic upscales 4×; Gaze draws the gaze prediction (at its horizon) on the bicubic frame; Foveated runs the SR model around the predicted gaze (blue arrow) on top of bicubic, and can add the gaze prediction and the fovea outline; VSR runs the SR model on the whole frame. The gaze model runs in its own process on 30 Hz samples of the decoded frames and the head pose. Parts that no enabled source uses are greyed out.
