    Predicted task

    Stream: frames sent per second vs the video's rate; upscaling time per frame; frame budget = share of each second spent working rather than waiting for the next frame slot (100% means the stream cannot keep up); late frames = sends that missed their slot. Gaze model: predictions per second (input is 30 Hz); model time in its process; round trip including the pipe; lag = how far the shown prediction is behind the newest frame; queue and drops if it falls behind. Threads & compute: CPU as a share of one core (the stream process includes all its threads; the model runs in its own process). MPS memory is GPU memory per process; GPU utilisation is not available on macOS without root. Updated twice a second.

    Pipelines

    Video Super Resolution

    Gaze-Contingent Video Super Resolution

    How a frame moves through the stream, with live values. Every NDI source comes from the same decoded frames: Source sends them as they are; Bicubic upscales 4×; Foveated runs the SR model around the predicted gaze (the gaze point arrow, at the step where the gaze will be after k frames) on top of bicubic, or with SR off sends bicubic as is, and can draw the full horizon gaze prediction and the fovea outline; VSR runs the SR model on the whole frame of every k-th frame (keyframes), and RIFE makes the frames in between from the two upscaled keyframes around them (not used at k = 1). The gaze model runs in its own process on 30 Hz samples of the decoded frames and the head pose. Press ▶ on an NDI box to stream that source alone (■ there stops it); while it streams, the parts it does not use are greyed out. Each area's options are under its diagram. Models stay loaded between runs.
